## Key Findings

**Financial crisis**: Stock down 66% from peak despite positive absolute revenue. Growth rate cut in half. Gross margins compressed 110bps. Americas (74% of revenue) declining.

**Competitive encroachment**: Vuori grew from 1.2% to 7.8% penetration among Lululemon customers. Customers now spend more at Vuori ($660) than Lululemon ($600). Alo Yoga jumped from 35th to 11th in teen rankings.

**Product & brand issues**: CEO admitted brand became "too predictable." Core leggings in outlet stores (unprecedented). Gen Z shifting to baggier silhouettes. Design cycle slower than competitors.

**Marketing opportunity**: Micro-influencers deliver 7.3x ROI. Podcast ads deliver 4.9x ROI with 62% host-read trust (vs 15% for social influencers). Both channels outperform traditional social media.
