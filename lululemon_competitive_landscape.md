# Lululemon Competitive Landscape Research Notes

## Executive Summary
Lululemon holds a strong market position but faces increasing competitive pressure from emerging upstart brands like Alo Yoga and Vuori, which are gaining market share through differentiated positioning, accessible pricing, and expanding retail networks. While Lululemon maintains a 21.2% market share in U.S. athleisure, competitors are growing faster and capturing Lululemon's own customers.

---

| Fabletics | 4.4% | Stable |

## Competitor Market Penetration & Growth

### Alo Yoga
- **Current market share:** 1.3% of U.S. athleisure spending
- **Market share growth:** +1% in last 12 months
- **Customer penetration:** Grew from 35th ranked brand with upper-income teens (Fall 2024) to 11th ranked (current)
- **Retail expansion:** 66 stores in U.S.
- **Lululemon customer overlap:** Earning wallet share from Lululemon customers

### Vuori
- **Current market share:** 2.9% of U.S. athleisure spending
- **Market share growth:** +1% in last 12 months
- **Valuation:** $5.5 billion (December 2024)
- **Lululemon customer migration:** Grew from 1.2% of Lululemon's customers shopping at Vuori (2018) to 7.8% (November 2024) - a 550% increase
- **Wallet share growth:** Vuori shoppers increased spending from 21.6% of athleisure wallet to 27.4% (year-over-year)
- **Store expansion plans:** Target 100 stores by 2026 (40 already open)
- **Piper Sandler Survey:** Vuori ranked 15th among upper-income teens, up from 24th in fall 2024

### On Holding
- **Premium footwear performance:** Achieved over 40% growth in performance footwear segment (2023-2024)
- **Market presence:** Less than 10% of Alo Yoga and Vuori shoppers cross-shop with On
- **Positioning:** Positioned as premium running/activewear with technical focus

### Athleta (Gap Inc. subsidiary)
- **Q4 FY2024 performance:** Net sales fell 5% to $396 million, comparable sales down 2%
- **Q2 FY2025 performance:** Net sales declined 11% to $300 million
- **Q1 FY2025 performance:** 6% sales decline due to product line adjustments
- **Retail footprint:** 300 stores at latest quarter (down from 338 a year ago)
- **Store closures:** U.S. count reduced to 290 from 327
- **Market share position:** Maintained but facing execution challenges
- **Strategic repositioning:** Hired Maggie Gauger from Nike (20-year veteran, former head of Nike's North American women's business)

### Nike
- **Global apparel market share 2024:** 2.9%
- **Projected 2025 share:** 2.6% (declining 0.3 percentage points)
- **Sneaker market leadership:** $33 billion in footwear sales
- **Combined with Adidas:** ~57% of global sneaker market

### Adidas
- **Global apparel market share 2024:** 1.8%
- **Projected 2025 share:** 1.9% (growing 0.1 percentage point)
- **Footwear sales:** $13 billion
- **Market momentum:** Gaining share from Nike in 2025

## Product Differentiation & Market Positioning

### Lululemon's Competitive Advantages
- **Technical performance focus:** Optimized for high-sweat, high-intensity workouts
- **Specialized expertise:** Deep focus on yoga, running, and athleisure
- **Pricing power:** 16.3% net margin vs Nike's 11.1%
- **Gross margin:** Stronger margins than Nike through premium pricing strategy
- **Core evergreen products:** Don't change with seasons, creating consistency
- **Direct-to-consumer strength:** Robust e-commerce platform, vertical integration
- **Marketing efficiency:** No extensive advertising campaigns; reliance on brand loyalty
- **Lifestyle positioning:** "Looking good while having a healthy lifestyle"

### Vuori's Competitive Advantages
- **All-day comfort positioning:** Transitions seamlessly from workout to travel to lounging
- **Versatile styling:** Less about pure athletic performance, more about everyday wearability
- **Sustainable materials:** DreamKnit™ fabric praised as "buttery-soft"
- **Differentiation strategy (per CEO Joe Kudla):** "Differentiated product, differentiated brand, differentiated store experience, differentiated materials"
- **Appeal to relaxed aesthetic:** Attracts consumers wanting understated, comfortable style
- **Rapid growth momentum:** 550% increase in Lululemon's customers shopping at Vuori (2018-2024)

### Alo Yoga's Competitive Advantages
- **Yoga-specific heritage:** Premium yoga apparel focus
- **Design aesthetic:** High-quality yoga wear with fashion-forward appeal
- **Sports bra offerings:** Specialized focus on women's athletic wear
- **Young brand positioning:** Appeal to trend-conscious consumers
- **Instagram/social presence:** Strong digital community and influencer partnerships

### Athleta's Positioning
- **Sustainability messaging:** Focus on environmental responsibility
- **Women-centric brand:** Targeted focus on female consumers
- **Broad retail footprint:** Wide store distribution (though declining)
- **Gap Inc. backing:** Parent company resources and distribution

## Lululemon Market Challenges

### Customer Switching & Churn
- **Americas comparable sales:** Down 5% year-over-year
- **Americas net revenue:** Down 2%
- **Consumer switching behavior:** Increased switching to competitors for value
- **Secondhand market growth:** U.S. secondhand apparel market grew 14% in 2024 (strongest growth since 2021)

### Competitive Pressures
- **Dupe culture impact:** #luludupes hashtag reached millions of TikTok views
- **Price sensitivity:** Consumers face financial strain from inflation and higher costs of living
- **Premium pricing challenge:** Difficulty maintaining $100+ pricing in current economy
- **Market saturation:** Flat U.S. results driven by saturation, price sensitivity, increased competition
- **Promotional activity:** Forced into competitive discounting with rising promotional pressure

### Market Trends Against Lululemon
- **Consumer value-seeking:** Guests hunting for deals and promotional offers
- **Emerging upstart appeal:** Younger brands gaining traction with teens and upper-income consumers
- **Market democratization:** Quality alternatives at lower prices becoming mainstream
- **Athleisure expansion:** New competitors entering and expanding product lines
