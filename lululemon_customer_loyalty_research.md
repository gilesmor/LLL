## Competitor Threat Analysis & Customer Switching

### Market Share Gains by Competitors
- **Vuori market share growth**: +1% since April 2023
- **Alo Yoga market share growth**: +1% since April 2023
- **Lululemon sales growth trend**: Declined from 30%+ to 10%
- **North American sales growth (Q1 2024)**: 3% (down from 17%)

### Customer Shopper Overlap (Wallet Share)
- **Alo Yoga shopper overlap with Lululemon**: 63%
- **Vuori shopper overlap with Lululemon**: 52%
- **Alo Yoga shopper overlap with Nike**: 34%
- **Vuori shopper overlap with Nike**: 30%

### Vuori Loyalty Growth
- **Vuori wallet share increase**: 27.4% of overall active/athleisure spending (up from 21.6% YoY)

### Reasons for Customer Switching

#### Price Sensitivity
- **Consumers planning to change shopping habits due to tariffs**: 82%
- **Tariff response behaviors**: Cutting back on nonessential spending, searching for sales/coupons, delaying purchases, switching to lower-priced retailers
- **Lululemon product price point**: Exceeding $100 per item (leggings, bras range $90-$130)

#### Product & Inventory Issues
- **Issue**: Insufficient sizes and colors in stock for younger customers
- **Impact**: Young shoppers redirected to competitors

#### Style/Trend Shifts
- **Demographic shift**: Gen Z preference for baggy pants vs. Lululemon's signature skin-tight look
- **Product innovation gap**: Loss of technical differentiation vs. competitors

#### Competitive Pressures
- **Alo Yoga brand strategy**: Monochromatic, chic sets with influencer backing
- **Vuori brand strategy**: Focus on men's athleticwear and versatility
- **Competitor expansion**: Both brands opening stores near existing Lululemon locations

### Regional Performance Challenges
- **U.S. market net revenue growth (Q3 2024)**: 9% (relatively flat)
- **International sales growth (Q3 2024)**: 33% increase
- **Primary headwinds**: Market saturation, price sensitivity, increased competition

## Summary & Key Takeaways

**Lululemon's customer base** remains concentrated among college-educated women aged 25-45 with household incomes exceeding $100,000. The brand has successfully built strong loyalty metrics with 83% self-identification as loyal customers and an NPS of 41, though this ranks 4th in its fashion/beauty peer group.

**However, critical headwinds** are evident: U.S. sales growth slowed to 9% in Q3 2024 with only 3% North American growth in Q1 2024 (down from 17%). Competitors like Vuori and Alo Yoga have captured growing market share (1% gains since April 2023) through influencer marketing and trend-responsive product design that appeals to Gen Z's preference for baggy, oversized silhouettes versus Lululemon's signature fitted aesthetic.

**Customer switching drivers** are multifaceted: 82% of consumers are actively changing shopping habits due to tariff concerns, price sensitivity is rising amid inflation, and inventory shortages of smaller sizes and colors have driven younger customers to competitors. The brand faces a loss of creative differentiation as its core bottoms category may have peaked in attracting new customers.

**Retention remains strong among high-value customers** (92% retention for top 20% customers) and overall retention rates of 72-76%, but acquisition challenges and competitive pressure suggest the loyalty ecosystem is under stress. Digital channels now represent 50% of revenue ($1.8B in Q4 2024), offering growth opportunity alongside the recovering men's segment (14% growth in 2024).
