# Lululemon's Recent Struggles: 2024-2026 Research

## Key Statistics & Financial Metrics

### Stock Performance Decline
- Stock down 66% from all-time high (early 2024 peak to late 2025)
- Stock down 57% in 2025 year-to-date
- Stock down approximately 25% in 2024
- Stock down 39-48% through mid-2025 periods
- Stock down 10% already in 2026 (early year performance)

### Revenue Performance
- 2025 Annual revenue: $10.588 billion (+10.07% YoY)
- 2024 Annual revenue: $9.619 billion (+18.6% YoY)
- 2023 Annual revenue: $8.127 billion
- **Growth Rate Deceleration:** 18.6% (2024) → 10.07% (2025) - MASSIVE slowdown
- FY2025 guidance: $11.15-$11.3 billion (5-7% growth)

### Profitability & Margins
- 2025 Net profit: $1.81 billion
- 2025 Profit margin: 17.14% (down from 17.1% prior year)
- 2025 Net profit margin: 15.7% (down from 17.1% previous year)
- Q2 2025 gross margin: 58.5% (down 110 basis points YoY from 59.6%)
- Q4 2024 gross margin: 60.4% (up 100 bps from Q4 2023's 59.4%)
- FY2024 gross margin: 59.2% (up 90 bps, but compressed in 2025)
- Operating margin expected to decline 100 basis points in 2025
- EPS growth projected to slow to just 2-3% in 2025

### Comparable Sales Trends
- Q3 FY2025: Americas comp sales down 5% (most recent quarter)
- Q3 FY2025: Global comp sales up 1% (or 2% constant currency)
- Q2 FY2025: Americas comp sales down 4% (or 3% constant currency)
- Q2 FY2025: Global comp sales up 1%
- Q4 FY2024: Comp sales up 3% (or 4% constant currency)
- FY2024 full year: Comp sales up 4%
- Q1 FY2024: Overall revenue growth slowed to 10% from 24% prior year
- Q1 FY2024: North American growth slowed to 3% from 17% prior year

### Geographic Performance Divergence
- Americas: 74% of total revenue but declining
- Americas comparable sales: DOWN 3-5% in recent quarters
- China Mainland comparable sales Q3 2025: UP 25%
- Rest of World comparable sales Q3 2025: UP 9%
- International comparable sales FY2024: UP 22%
- International growth masking Americas' domestic stagnation

### Inventory Issues
- Q2 2025: Inventory up 21% YoY to $1.7 billion
- Core black leggings appeared in outlet stores (summer 2024) - unprecedented
- High volume of men's basics and polos on sale (Q2 2024)
- Stock-outs in small women's sizes (Q1 2024)
- Inventory builds for seasonal purposes but slower domestic sell-through

## Market Saturation & Competitive Pressures

### North America Market Status
- US and Canada markets showing signs of stagnation
- Market saturation evidence across North America
- Increased price sensitivity among consumers
- Heavy competition intensifying in core markets

### Competitive Share Loss
- Vuori customer penetration: Grew from 1.2% (2018) to 7.8% (late 2025) of Lululemon's customer base
- Vuori market share in $200k+ household segment: 15.31% (2025) vs 11.87% (2023)
- Lululemon market share in $200k+ households: 57% (still dominant but losing ground)
- Vuori spending share: Shoppers increased wallet share to 27.4% (from 21.6% prior year)
- Vuori customer overlap with Lululemon: 52%
- Alo Yoga customer overlap with Lululemon: 63%
- Vuori growth rate: 23% through end of October 2025
- Vuori valuation: $5.5 billion (as of late 2024/early 2025)
- Vuori pricing: 2-3 notches below Lululemon for comparable products

### Product & Design Issues
- Breezethrough leggings pulled from retail (July 2024) due to unflattering V-shaped back seam and sheer material complaints
- Shift in fashion trends toward baggier silhouettes - Lululemon missed this trend
- Core products (Align pant, Softstreme fleece) haven't evolved much
- Company acknowledged becoming "too predictable within casual offerings" (CEO Calvin McDonald, September 2024)
- Loss of trendsetters and cultural relevance, especially with Gen Z consumers
- Design cycle slower than competitors
- Brand losing favor among younger consumers preferring looser fits

## Leadership & Strategic Issues

### CEO Departure
- Calvin McDonald announced departure effective January 31, 2026
- Meghan Frank (CFO) and André Maestrini (Chief Commercial Officer) announced as interim co-CEOs
- Departure follows 15+ months of underperformance
- Founder Chip Wilson waged public campaign against McDonald, including full-page WSJ advertisement
- Founder criticized loss of brand "cool" and "brand erosion"
- Demands for strategic reset and innovation

### Key Business Headwinds
1. **Tariff & Supply Chain Inflation:** Primary driver of 2025 gross margin compression
2. **Fashion Trend Misalignment:** Failed to adapt to baggier silhouettes and Gen Z preferences
3. **Product Innovation Stagnation:** Core products not evolving
4. **Market Saturation:** North America hitting growth ceiling
5. **Inventory Mismanagement:** Clearance issues requiring heavy markdowns
6. **Competitive Encroachment:** Vuori and Alo Yoga gaining share rapidly
7. **Consumer Perception:** Brand seen as "predictable" and losing cool factor

## Quarterly Performance Breakdown

### Q3 FY2025 (Quarter ended December 5, 2025)
- Americas comparable sales: -5%
- Global comparable sales: +1% (or +2% constant currency)
- China comparable sales: +25%
- Rest of World comparable sales: +9%

### Q2 FY2025 (Quarter ended August 3, 2025)
- Gross profit: $1.5 billion (58.5% of revenue)
- Gross margin: DOWN 110 basis points YoY (from 59.6%)
- Americas comparable sales: -4% (or -3% constant currency)
- Global comparable sales: +1%
- Rest of World comparable sales: +12% (or +9% constant currency)

### Q4 FY2024 (Quarter ended February 1, 2025)
- Gross profit: $2.2 billion (60.4% of revenue)
- Comparable sales: +3% (or +4% constant currency)

### Q1 FY2024 (Quarter ended May 4, 2024)
- Overall revenue growth: 10% (down from 24% prior year)
- North American growth: 3% (down from 17% prior year)

## Timeline of Key Events

- **Q1 2024 (May 2024):** North American growth slows dramatically to 3%
- **Summer 2024 (July 2024):** Breezethrough leggings pulled due to quality/fit issues
- **Q2 2024 (August 2024):** Stock price down 25%+ as inventory concerns mount
- **September 2024:** CEO admits company has "become too predictable"
- **Q3 2024:** Americas comp sales decline 2%
- **Late 2024:** Founder Chip Wilson launches public campaign against leadership
- **Q4 2024 (February 2025):** Full year FY2024 shows 18.6% revenue growth but slowing pace
- **Q1 2025 (May 2025):** Stock down 57% YTD
- **Q2 2025 (August 2025):** Gross margin compression accelerates; inventory builds to $1.7B (+21% YoY)
- **Q3 2025 (December 2025):** Americas comp sales down 5%; CEO departure announced
- **December 11, 2025:** Calvin McDonald departure announced effective January 31, 2026
- **Early 2026:** Stock down additional 10%+ as leadership uncertainty continues
